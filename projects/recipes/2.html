Origin of the dish:

Relleno de papa is a traditional dish found in Bolivia and other latin American countries. It consists of mashed potatoes wrapped around a filling of meat loin, sausage and boiled egg. The outside is coated and fried until golden brown. Different families have their own way of making this but this how my family always makes it.
