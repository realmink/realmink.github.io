Bolivian Pique Macho

A Taste of Bolivia

Pique Macho is a popular Bolivian dish made with beef, sausages, french fries, onions, Bell peppers and tomatoes. Most of the time this dish is topped off with Ketchup, Mustard, Mayonnaise, and a spicy sauce called, Llajwa . (this photo was taken by me, i own it. you may not use it.)

Origin of the dish:

Pique Macho is said to have originated in 1974 at El Prado restaurant in Cochabamba, Bolivia. According to the local story, two men came to the restaurant asking for a filling meal and so the restaurants staff assembled a hearty platter of beef, sausage, onions, bell peppers, tomatoes, and french fries. The dish was spicy, so one popular version of the story says the customers joked that it was a meal "for real men"- "Este es un picado para Machos!". You might ask, "what does that even mean!?" well, pique refers to "picado", chopped. And Macho just means manly. the name plays on the idea that the meal is so hearty and spicy that you have to be macho to eat it. But of course anyone can eat it.

Llajwa

this is the spicy side sauce we use for almost every food we even put it in soups dude.

  1. get your Locoto, Tomato, and quilquiña
  2. wash all of these
  3. youll need to locotos to cut both of them in half and take out the seeds.
  4. in the blender you'll put the two locotos with all the seeds out, a whole tomato cut in two, and the quilquiña. then youll add a bit of water and blend it.
  5. your llajwa shouldn't be all liquid it should be at least a bit thick.
  6. this parts optional but we put small sliced cube like onion peices
  7. then finally, add salt to your liking.

Originally in my country we used Rocks instead of blenders. one rock for the base and the other rock to mush the vegtables together. This is called a Batan
